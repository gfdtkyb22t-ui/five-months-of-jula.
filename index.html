To my Jula, my Juli, my Julia. ❤️

I honestly don't even know where to start. Five whole months of having you in my life, and I genuinely couldn't be happier that I get to call you mine. Ever since 19/06/26, you've become such a special part of my life, and I wouldn't change that for anything.

I still remember when I finally asked you out and you said, "finally you asked me out." And before you even start, YES YOU DID SAY THAT. I don't care how many times you deny it. 😭 It's funny how even the smallest moments with you become memories I never want to forget.

I love all the little things about you: the way you talk, the way you laugh, the things that make you excited, and even the random things you do that probably don't mean much to you but mean everything to me. I love how you can be so effortlessly yourself, your Pinterest dress obsession, your love for white orchids, and your collection of Fugglers (even though I still question your taste in some of those creatures).

Even our stupid little jokes, including the way I pronounce certain words because apparently being British is a crime in your eyes, are things I wouldn't trade for anything. And yes, I know you love my ass, you don't exactly make that difficult to work out. 😭

I hope you know how much you mean to me, Juli. I hope I make you feel as appreciated, valued and loved as you deserve to feel. These five months are only one little chapter of everything I hope we get to experience together. I want more memories, more laughs, more silly arguments about things neither of us will admit, more pictures, more dates and more moments where we can just be ourselves.

Thank you for being you, thank you for being in my life, and thank you for making these past five months so special. I love you so much, my girl. Happy five months, Julia. Here's to us. ❤️

Always yours, ♡
